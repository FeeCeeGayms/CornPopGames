THE SNACK ARSENAL
Five more weapons are hidden around the maps: 5 FIZZ LAUNCHER (bouncing cola cans that fizz up and pop near enemies) · 6 SAUCE GUN (a hot marinara stream that slows enemies) · 7 SPAGHETTI RAILGUN (one noodle goes through everything in a line) · 8 JELLY BLASTER (rapid jelly blobs; shoot your feet to jelly-jump) · 9 BIG FONDUE GUN (a cheese ball that zaps everything near it, then erupts)

MOVEMENT
Quake III Arena physics: keep pressing forward plus a strafe direction and turn smoothly while jumping to strafe-jump faster than you can run. Release and press jump again for each hop (or turn on Auto bunny-hop in Options).

GOAL
Guide CornPop through the Pidser Cave. Grab the RED, BLUE and GOLD keycards to open matching doors, and reach the swirling exit gate. Grab the Super Soaker on the street first: Qoqs throw chickens (4 damage each) and chase you once they spot you. Yellow juice jugs refill the soaker, pizza boxes heal 25 and slices heal 5.
